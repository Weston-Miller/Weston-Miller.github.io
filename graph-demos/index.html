Companion to Graded Ehrhart Theory for Hypersimplices. Draw a loopless multigraph G below; each tab runs a different construction from the paper on it. Click empty space to add a vertex, click two vertices to connect (click a pair again for a parallel edge); switch the mode button to delete. You can also type the edge list directly.
