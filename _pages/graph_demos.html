Hypersimplex harmonics — interactive demos

Companion to Graded Ehrhart Theory for Hypersimplices. Draw a loopless multigraph G below; each tab runs a different construction from the paper on it. Click empty space to add a vertex, click two vertices to connect (click a pair again for a parallel edge). The Ehrhart tab uses only ℓ and n.

Section 4: the graded q-Ehrhart series in closed rational form (Theorem 4.2 denominator), its interior series, and the q-reciprocity check. Uses ℓ and n.
