## [Rational Catalan numbers](/rational-catalan/)

Companion to *Rational Catalan numbers for complex reflection groups* ([J. Algebra, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2025.01.027)).
Builds the same number from both ends: counted off *(a, b)*-Dyck paths you can see, and computed from the
canonical symmetrizing trace on the Hecke algebra at a power of a Coxeter element. Vary the group and the
parameter and watch the two sides stay equal.

## [Cayley's formula](/cayley/)

Companion to *An elaborate new proof of Cayley's formula* ([Algebraic Combinatorics, 2025](https://doi.org/10.5802/alco.429)).
Pick a vertex-labelled tree on *n* vertices and see it three ways at once — as a cyclic embedding, as the
cyclic factorization read off by walking clockwise from vertex *n*, and as the corresponding maximal
distinguished subword. Edit any one of the three and the others follow.

## [Hypersimplex harmonics](/graph-demos/)

Companion to *Graded Ehrhart theory for hypersimplices* ([arXiv, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27438)).
Draw a loopless multigraph — click to add vertices, click two to join them — and each tab runs a different
construction from the paper on it. You can also type the edge list directly.
